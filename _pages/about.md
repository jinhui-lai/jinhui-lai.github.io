# About Me

Hi! My name is Jinhui Lai (赖锦辉). I am a PhD student at the [GOOD Lab](https://good.ncu.edu.cn) in Nanchang University, supervised by Professor [Zichen Xu](https://good.ncu.edu.cn/lab_chair). Since October 2024, I have been a visiting student at [WingTecher Lab](http://wingtecher.com/homeen) , Tsinghua University, advised by Professor  [Yu Jiang](https://sites.google.com/site/jiangyu198964/home).  My research is focused on DBMS fuzzing. I have detected over 200 bugs in widely used DBMSs (e.g., MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL, SQLite, and TiDB), including crashes, logic bugs, and performance bugs.
